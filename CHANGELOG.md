# @spon/payload-notifications

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 177a33c: Initial release.

  A notifications plugin for Payload CMS that adds rule-driven notification events,
  subscriptions, and an inbox. Ships built-in email, inbox, and webhook channels
  (with SSRF defence-in-depth on webhook URLs), immediate and daily/weekly digest
  delivery jobs with at-most-once delivery guarantees, configurable field
  conditions and triggers, and overridable collection slugs and fields.
